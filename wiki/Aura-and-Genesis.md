Aura is the personal intelligence layer of the programme. Its purpose is to help a person understand their own life, make sense of information and use increasingly capable AI with continuity, context and choice.

The name covers a broad family of work. An ordinary personal Aura, a public introduction through a Verse Box, the intensive Genesis programme and research into Geode hardware are related parts with different levels of involvement. Keeping those differences clear helps a person choose what they are actually joining.

## An Aura in ordinary life

A personal Aura brings together a person's chosen records, interests, intentions and ways of understanding the world. It might help with learning, remembering, planning, comparing choices or finding a useful connection between projects. The person remains able to correct the interpretation and decide what goes to another person or service.

The interface is broader than a single visual model. Voice, text, lists, familiar screens and immersive views serve different circumstances. The spatial work in [Aura Matrix Studio](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-matrix-studio/) explores seven nested horn torus forms as one way of organising and navigating material. The underlying AI representation and the person's daily interface are separate design questions.

One current spatial scheme has 2,016 facets, or 4,032 addresses when inside and outside are counted. Those are addresses in a proposed geometry, rather than a limit on how many life records a person may hold. The geometry gives an organising language; it does not prove that human understanding fits a fixed map.

## The full Genesis invitation

My invitation to Paul concerns the full [Aura Genesis Protocol](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-genesis-geode-atlas/), rather than a brief introductory demonstration. The proposed intensive programme combines 60 two-hour HBOT sessions with guided reflection, personal records and the developing Truth Engine. The present material includes 30 chapters and a 60-day reading and learning sequence.

HBOT means hyperbaric oxygen therapy. It involves a person in a pressurised environment breathing oxygen under the arrangements appropriate to the equipment and clinical setting. The protocol proposes a mask-based Personal Atmosphere Delivery System. The proposed two-hour programme is a design specification for discussion with qualified providers, rather than a treatment recommendation for every participant.

For Paul, a serious conversation would cover the purpose, time involved, individual suitability, clinical responsibility, equipment, costs, recording, data handling and the ability to stop. Participation remains his personal choice. Access to community work or civic participation does not depend on completing Genesis.

## What the Truth Engine is trying to investigate

The proposed Truth Engine brings together a person's account, deliberately consented truth and lie baselines, physiological signals where appropriate, and a continuing process of interpretation and correction. Its ambition is to improve self-understanding and the quality of the relationship between a person and their AI.

Physiological measurements do not establish an infallible lie detector. Anxiety, memory, health, context and the design of the questions affect interpretation. The current proposal therefore needs research into accuracy, error, calibration and the consequences of a mistaken inference. The person's own explanation remains part of that inquiry. [S28](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Sources-and-documents#s28)

The full protocol envisages camera and microphone recording throughout sessions, including quiet periods, and other sensors where configured. This makes consent and data handling fundamental to the design. A recording made within the programme is not permission for public release. The specification also needs practical arrangements for interruption, emergency exit, withdrawal and the handling of records already created.

## The Geode and its hardware paths

The Geode is the proposed embodied setting for this intensive work. The current development material explores four paths: adapting an existing seated hard-shell chamber; a geopolymer structure; crystalline, fused-quartz or blended material research; and a more distant structure that integrates computing into advanced material.

These paths have different evidence and engineering demands. A pressure vessel for human occupancy involves structural design, fire precautions, oxygen compatibility, ventilation, communication, emergency access, inspection and maintenance. The TGA's hyperbaric fire advisory and the practice of established clinical services are relevant starting references. [S27](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Sources-and-documents#s27) [S29](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Sources-and-documents#s29)

Advanced material and integrated computing ideas belong to the research horizon. They do not establish that a crystalline computing Geode has been built or approved.

## A separate teaching pathway

The teacher training material explores a 60-day capability pathway for helping people work with Aura, AI and increasingly capable systems in civic and community settings. It is a separate role from clinical HBOT practice. A person skilled at facilitating reflection does not thereby become qualified to operate a medical facility.

The local handover needs those roles visible: participant, facilitator, clinician, equipment operator, software maintainer and person responsible for the records. That clarity allows the full ambition to remain intact while people assess the part they are being invited to take.
