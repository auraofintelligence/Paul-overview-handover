## Terms used in this wiki

| Term | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| Agency | A person's real freedom and capacity to choose and act in their own life |
| AI | Artificial intelligence, including the current tools and future systems being considered |
| Alignment | Work on the relationship between a system's behaviour, human intentions, values and consequences |
| Aura | The developing personal intelligence environment and related family of projects |
| [C-Hour](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/C-Hours-and-reciprocity) | One verified hour of voluntary human contribution, with no fixed financial value or redemption promise |
| Chaos oracle | A developing approach in which algorithms alter priorities as circumstances and opportunities change, with room for serendipity and the traveller's own choice |
| Co-design | Developing a proposal with the people who use, carry or are affected by it |
| CYOA | Choose Your Own Adventure: optional paths from the first step, with non-binding exploration |
| Cultural authority | The actual entitlement and responsibility to decide about particular cultural knowledge and its use |
| [Cultural Intelligence Node](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/ready-set-co-op-cultural-intelligence-node) | The proposed combination of local computing, people, records, learning and shared work |
| [GAJRA Earth](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/GAJRA-earth-infinity) | Global Association for Joyful Responsible Abundance on Earth, a proposed network of locally rooted associations |
| Generation city | A proposed settlement with the capability to sustain worthwhile life across successive generations |
| Genesis | The proposed intensive Aura programme combining reflection, HBOT and [Truth Engine](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Aura-and-Genesis#what-the-truth-engine-is-trying-to-investigate) development |
| Geode | The proposed embodied chamber and setting for intensive Genesis work, with several hardware research paths |
| HBOT | Hyperbaric oxygen therapy, with its individual clinical and equipment requirements |
| JRA | Joyful Responsible Abundance, the programme's guiding inquiry into worthwhile lives, consequences and shared capability |
| Living twin | A developing representation of a place and its processes, updated through selected records and observations |
| [Mutual Futures](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/mutual-futures/) | The central shared-ownership, succession, livelihood, learning and time proposal |
| [P4A](https://p4a.xyz/) | Purple Party for Australia: currently a website and public civic workbench, with an objective to seed and trigger a national Australian Cyber Republic referendum on or before 2031 |
| Ready S.E.T. | Sustainable Employment and Training, the proposed cooperative work and capability layer |
| SI | Superintelligence, used here for a possible future capability substantially beyond present systems |
| Truth Engine | A proposed, research-stage approach to self-understanding and calibrated interpretation; its accuracy remains to be established |
| [Verse Box](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/aura-verse-box) | A proposed public interface for introductions to Aura, learning, contribution and participation |
| Workbench | A public tool or structured place for exploring evidence, choices and designs |
| XR | Extended reality, including immersive and augmented interfaces where useful |
