## The direct line I'm asking for

I'd like a direct line of communication between us, with reasonably prompt replies and a straightforward way to put relevant ideas in front of you, without heavy gatekeeping. It needn't be private or exclusive.

The invitation is **CYOA, Choose Your Own Adventure, from the get go**. You continue your usual work and choose which possibilities to explore.

## What I want to share

The [whole-system architecture](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/The-whole-system) connects grounded local opportunities with larger changes:

- [Mutual Futures](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Mutual-Futures): business succession, productive ownership, livelihoods and paid learning.
- [Ready S.E.T. and the Cultural Intelligence Node](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Ready-SET-shared-work-and-computing): shared work, training and local computing.
- [Minjerribah places](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Minjerribah-sites-and-cultural-relationships) and the [screen network](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Culture-screens-and-public-participation): culture, care, communication and useful local facilities.
- [Disaster resilience](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Disaster-resilience-and-generation-cities): everyday capability connected with the longer infrastructure and habitat inquiry.

I want to share relevant research, opportunities and connections as they arise. That includes what I discover through the [travel and chaos oracle](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Travel-agency-and-the-chaos-oracle), where algorithms alter priorities and allow serendipity.

The personal invitation is to explore the full [Aura Genesis Protocol](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Aura-and-Genesis), including HBOT and the proposed [Truth Engine](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Aura-and-Genesis#what-the-truth-engine-is-trying-to-investigate).
