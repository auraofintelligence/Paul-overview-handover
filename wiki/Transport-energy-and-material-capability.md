Transport, energy, computing and materials form the physical capability behind many of the programme's social goals. People need to reach work and care, goods need to arrive, equipment needs reliable power, and useful assets need maintenance throughout their life.

The local proposals connect these needs with ownership, training and resilience. Each has its own business and engineering questions. The council handover would help establish a sensible sequence and the people responsible for developing it.

## Moreton Bay mobility

The [Moreton Bay Autonomous Mobility](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/moreton-bay-autonomous-mobility/index.html) proposal explores regional passenger movement, goods delivery and air services between mainland and island locations. The aim is wider access across the day and year, where technology, approvals, conditions and a viable operation permit it.

The programme includes hubs, charging, maintenance, recovery and local capability. Existing operators and workers belong in the ownership and transition discussion. An automated vehicle still sits within an operation with responsibilities to passengers, staff, the public and the environment.

An early study would choose a specific journey and compare the real alternatives: travel time, reliability, accessibility, fare, staffing, maintenance, energy, weather limits and the connection at each end. A supplier's demonstration elsewhere would be useful evidence, with its conditions made clear. It would not establish approval for a Redlands route.

## The ferry terminal is one related public decision

The Queensland Government's Dunwich Ferry Terminal Upgrade has a confirmed **$41 million** commitment through the South East Queensland City Deal. As at the TMR update of 29 September 2026, the business case was due in late 2026 and planning was expected to continue into 2027. The public consultation ran from 28 May to 21 June 2026. [S19](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Sources-and-documents#s19) [S20](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Sources-and-documents#s20)

My [Dunwich / Gumpi Open Data Lab](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/dunwich-gumpi-ferry-terminal-open-data-lab/) is developing evidence and a competing approach for consideration. It explores site records, public data, visualisation and simulation. It is not yet a complete independently costed alternative business case.

The handover's purpose is the wider relationship and programme. The terminal is one concrete place where better public evidence, access and connected planning matter. The consultation report also makes clear that consolidation of ferry services is outside the upgrade's project scope. A wider proposal would therefore need its own pathway.

## Energy as a shared local capability

[Straddie Clean Energy Superpower](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/straddie-clean-energy-superpower) brings together solar generation, batteries, heat, thermal storage and research into other resources and technologies. Wave and tidal energy, wind concepts, hydrogen and mineral opportunities appear as questions to examine, with differing levels of maturity.

The practical inquiry begins with demand, land and ecological conditions, connection options, storage duration, maintenance and cost. A project needs to say whether it supports a building, a service, a local network or export. Those uses have different requirements and revenue arrangements.

Mutual Futures adds an ownership question: how would the local benefits be held and shared? The proposed cooperative adds a capability question: who learns to operate and maintain it? The disaster work adds a continuity question: what still functions during an outage?

## Excavation, corridors and material use

[Sandworm Subterranean Systems](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/sandworm-subterranean-systems) explores underground transport and utility corridors with possible uses for excavated material. Geopolymer products, manufactured forms, heat storage and other ideas need actual sampling, testing and environmental assessment.

Sand and other excavated material vary. A useful manufacturing process depends on composition, processing, inputs, product quality, energy, transport, waste and a market or public use for the result. An attractive diagram of a circular system leaves those measurements to be done.

The staged proposition is to develop useful infrastructure and material capability first, then use what is learned to judge the next step. [The generation cities chapter](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Disaster-resilience-and-generation-cities) explains the much larger horizon that these early capabilities might support.

## A place for grants and practical support

The [Stradbroke Grants Lab](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/stradbroke-grants-lab/) helps connect a defined project with a suitable funding opportunity, evidence, budget and reporting. Funding windows change, and eligibility belongs to the actual applicant and programme.

For the handover, a useful early contribution would be a shared project brief for each active proposal: purpose, current evidence, responsible people, next decision and resources needed. That makes it easier for council and other participants to help without being asked to approve the whole physical vision at once.
