Minjerribah is home and the starting place for this programme's local expression. The proposed network brings together culture, living, learning, care, making and shared infrastructure across several places. Each place has its own people, history, authority and practical conditions.

I am developing part of this work with a local Indigenous clever man who is Oodgeroo Noonuccal, Kath Walker's, grandson. That relationship matters to the work. It does not make me a cultural authority or establish agreement from every Quandamooka person, family or organisation.

The [multi-site Minjerribah network](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/multi-site-Minjerribah-network) sets out the current proposal. It is a starting point for place-specific agreements, rather than a single agreement covering all land, services and cultural material.

## Different places, different purposes

- **[7-Mile](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/multi-site-Minjerribah-network/seven-mile.html):** Noonuccal-led living, cultural activity, gardens, making, learning and residencies.
- **[82 Claytons Road, Amity](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/Anglican-Diocese-82-Claytons-Amity/):** proposed gift and partnership involving aged care, disability support and Genesis-related research.
- **[9 Ballow Road Trust Hub](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/multi-site-Minjerribah-network):** proposed home for shared working arrangements.
- **[10-12 Ballow Road Sand and Screen Hub](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/ballow-road-sand-screen-hub/):** proposed sport, film, learning, gatherings and intergenerational activity.
- **Recovery and support places:** separate proposals including men's recovery and support for women and children.

These proposals have connections, but they are separate commitments. The [9 Ballow Road Trust Hub](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/multi-site-Minjerribah-network) and the [10-12 Ballow Road](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/ballow-road-sand-screen-hub/) Sand and Screen Hub are different concepts. A promise or permission for one does not apply automatically to the other.

## 7-Mile and Indigenous majority ownership

The working commercial proposal for [7-Mile](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/multi-site-Minjerribah-network/seven-mile.html) includes a 41/39/20 ownership split that retains an Indigenous majority. The broader direction also includes a purpose-specific not-for-profit structure with Indigenous majority control. The final arrangements would need the participating people's own decisions and proper documentation.

That proposed split belongs to the specified venture. It is not a formula imposed on every site or a claim over Country. The rights and responsibilities connected with Country, cultural knowledge and family relationships are not company assets created by an ownership chart.

## 82 Claytons Road

I ask the Diocese to gift [82 Claytons Road, Amity](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/Anglican-Diocese-82-Claytons-Amity/) to a community fund to be established, provisionally called the **Straddie Sovereign Wealth Fund**, and help shape a partnership for dementia support, aged care and longevity research. The proposed fund would hold assets for lasting island benefit, with land and long-term assets potentially held separately from operating budgets.

The updated proposal connects [whole of life Aura support](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Aura-and-Genesis#adaptive-whole-of-life-support) with memory palaces, intergenerational learning and specialist research. It invites conversations with Anglicare, NSI Housing and its existing care services, Yulu-Burri-Ba and Minjerribah Moorgumpin Elders-in-Council. These are prospective invitations, with the property gift and partnerships still proposed. [Background document D24](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Sources-and-documents#supplied-background-documents).
