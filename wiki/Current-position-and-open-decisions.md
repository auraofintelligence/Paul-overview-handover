## P4A's present position

[P4A, Purple Party for Australia](https://p4a.xyz/), is a website and public civic workbench. I have undertaken no P4A outreach and have no confirmed support from anyone. It is not a registered party and has no party members.

Its objective is to seed and trigger a national Australian referendum on establishing a Cyber Republic on or before 2031. The [P4A chapter](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/P4A-law-and-democratic-participation) explains the proposed direction.
