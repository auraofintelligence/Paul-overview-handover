## Where evidence is still needed

Mutual Futures needs willing participants, enterprise information, a chosen legal and ownership structure, and a properly resourced operating model. A site proposal needs land and cultural agreements, design work and an operating group. A computing node needs actual workloads and a credible support and cost comparison.

Genesis needs its clinical, research, equipment and information arrangements. The Truth Engine needs evidence about its performance and errors. Material and underground infrastructure ideas need testing and site-specific engineering. The solar research needs clear mechanisms, distinguishing observations and review of competing accounts.

Each of those is a next piece of work, with a different kind of evidence. They are recorded here so an interested person can see where their skill or relationship would be useful.

## Keeping the record current

The public roles, project status, source versions and accepted responsibilities would be reviewed when something material changes. A short dated change note would explain what changed and why. Older documents retain their history while this overview gives the current explanation.

The first practical update would record the outcome of the conversation with Paul. That is the point at which this proposed handover begins to become an agreed working relationship.
