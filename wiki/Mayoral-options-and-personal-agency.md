I have considered the possibility of standing for Mayor of Redland City if a vacancy creates a by-election before the final part of the present term. I believe this body of work gives me the substance for a serious public case and the capacity to build a winning campaign. That is my assessment of my capacity and the programme, rather than a polling result or an announced candidacy.

My preference is a local leadership arrangement with Paul and other willing people. I want to keep developing the wider architecture, earn a living, travel and build relationships beyond the region. A council position would consume a substantial part of that freedom and attention.

## The date that changes the process

The relevant threshold is **16 March 2027**. The last ordinary Queensland local government election was on 16 March 2024. Under sections 161 and 164 of the Local Government Act 2009, the final part of the term begins on the third anniversary of that election. [S17](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Sources-and-documents#s17) [S18](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Sources-and-documents#s18)

| When the mayoral office becomes vacant | Current route for filling it |
|---|---|
| Before 16 March 2027 | A by-election for mayor |
| From 16 March 2027 to the next ordinary election | Council appoints one of its existing councillors by resolution |

The date the office becomes vacant determines the route. The date on which a later poll could be held is a separate matter. The current statutory framework is the basis for this overview; any actual vacancy would need its circumstances and the law then in force checked.

There is no assumption here that the present mayor will be removed or that a vacancy will occur.

## Acting as mayor and becoming mayor

The Deputy Mayor performs the mayor's functions in the circumstances provided by the Act. Councillors may also perform acting duties at particular times. Seeing Paul, Julie or another councillor representing the mayor at an event does not by itself establish a right to the office after a vacancy.

During the final part of the term, the appointment comes through a council resolution selecting an existing councillor. That gives the existing council a defined role in the outcome. Public relationships and the ability to work with colleagues matter, alongside any individual's interest in serving.

## What a short term would involve

A by-election winner serves the balance of the term, rather than receiving a fresh four-year term. With the next ordinary election in 2028, the remaining period could be around a year, depending on when the vacancy and election occur.

That period would include the duties of the office, council relationships, existing budgets and programmes, public expectations and preparation for the next election. It would offer a platform for local leadership, with a substantial workload and a limited period to establish continuity.

My consideration has included that trade-off. The programme reaches well beyond a council term. Winning office would be one possible way to serve it, with an opportunity cost for the wider work. A handover offers another route if the local people accepting responsibility have a viable arrangement.

## What I want Paul to understand

I am bringing a developed body of work, a long period of investigation and a willingness to make a public case. I am also making a deliberate choice about where my effort is most useful. The preference for a handover comes from the scale and direction of the work and the kind of life I want to retain.

This conversation is not an offer to transfer an electoral mandate. Voters choose a mayor at a by-election; councillors make the appointment provided by the Act during the final part of the term. Any campaign or council appointment would have its own public process.

The practical invitation stands whether or not a vacancy arises. Paul could help convene the local programme in his present role, alongside other councillors and community participants. A durable working group, resources and accepted responsibilities would be useful under any mayoral outcome.

## The immediate decision

The first decision is whether Paul wants to explore that local leadership relationship. From there we could agree the part he would carry, the people to involve, the support required and the way my ongoing role would work.
