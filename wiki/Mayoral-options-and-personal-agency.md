I have considered the possibility of standing for Mayor of Redland City if a vacancy creates a by-election before the final part of the present term. I believe this body of work gives me the substance for a serious public case and the capacity to build a winning campaign.

My preference is to share useful local possibilities with you and other interested people while I keep developing the wider architecture, earn a living and travel. You would keep doing your usual work and choose which inputs to explore. A council position would consume a substantial part of the freedom and attention I want for that wider life.

## The date that changes the process

The relevant threshold is **16 March 2027**. The last ordinary Queensland local government election was on 16 March 2024. Under sections 161 and 164 of the [Local Government Act 2009](https://www.legislation.qld.gov.au/view/whole/html/current/act-2009-017), the final part of the term begins on the third anniversary of that election. [S17](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Sources-and-documents#s17) [S18](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Sources-and-documents#s18)

| When the mayoral office becomes vacant | Current route for filling it |
|---|---|
| Before 16 March 2027 | A by-election for mayor |
| From 16 March 2027 to the next ordinary election | Council appoints one of its existing councillors by resolution |

The date the office becomes vacant determines the route. The date on which a later poll could be held is a separate matter. The current statutory framework is the basis for this overview; any actual vacancy would need its circumstances and the law then in force checked.

## Acting as mayor and becoming mayor

The Deputy Mayor performs the mayor's functions in the circumstances provided by the Act. Councillors may also perform acting duties at particular times. Seeing you, Julie or another councillor representing the mayor at an event does not by itself establish a right to the office after a vacancy.

## What a short term would involve

A by-election winner serves the balance of the term, rather than receiving a fresh four-year term. With the next ordinary election in 2028, the remaining period could be around a year, depending on when the vacancy and election occur.
