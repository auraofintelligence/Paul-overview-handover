**P4A means Purple Party for Australia.** My objective is to seed and trigger a **national Australian referendum, on or before 2031, on establishing a Cyber Republic**, with the resulting system demonstrated to the world around Brisbane 2032.

As at **25 October 2026**, P4A is still only a website and public civic workbench. I have undertaken no outreach for P4A and have no confirmed support from anyone. It is not a registered political party and has no party members. My meetings about other local projects do not establish support for P4A.

The public site is [p4a.xyz](https://p4a.xyz/). The [source repository](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/p4a-xyz-cinema) contains its developing tools and public examples. The intended path is to help seed a grassroots movement towards the constitutional objective.

Participation begins as CYOA, Choose Your Own Adventure. A visitor chooses what to read, question, compare or explore. Browsing or trying the workbench is non-binding and creates no membership or political commitment.

## From the 2012 law inquiry to a public workbench

My [Australian Law 2012 collection](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/australian-law-2012-lukes-relevance/index.html) records an early period of investigating how law, institutions and a person's circumstances fit together. The later Constitutional Matrix, Aura and civic work continue that interest in making complex arrangements visible and open to examination.

The thread matters to this handover because the local programme raises public questions as well as operating questions. A transport proposal involves evidence, access, money and public decisions. A community computing node involves information, authority and ongoing cost. A business succession arrangement affects workers and residents as well as its owners.

P4A seeks to give those relationships an intelligible public record. It does not assume that a larger collection of information automatically produces a fair decision.

## What the workbench holds

The present architecture connects public claims and evidence with participation, outcomes, legal memory and change over time. Its Twinkles are structured prompts and perspectives for exploring the subject from several directions. Their purpose is to make the inquiry more accessible and reveal questions a single summary might leave out.

The Redlands example connects the city's ten divisions with local project seeds and dated indicators. The website also includes material across all eight states and territories, with differing levels of detail. That is the scope of the published workbench. There is no established P4A membership or outreach network behind those pages.

A useful local record would let a person ask:

1. What is being proposed, and by whom?
2. What evidence supports it and what remains uncertain?
3. Who has the actual power to decide?
4. What would it cost, who pays, and who benefits?
5. How do affected people contribute or challenge it?
6. What happened after the decision, and what changed?

## Personal and public information

A civic profile is a chosen public representation. It is distinct from the much richer private material someone may keep in a personal Aura. Sharing a view about a local project does not give the project access to a person's health records or private life.

AI can help compare documents, locate provisions, summarise submissions and show conflicting accounts. The source and date remain important. A useful system makes it easy to inspect the original and correct a mistaken interpretation. Earlier descriptions of flawless legal AI exceed what the present tools establish.

## The constitutional horizon

The Cyber Republic objective concerns the design of democratic participation and public representation as intelligence systems become more capable. The referendum is the proposed national constitutional pathway, on or before 2031. Brisbane 2032 is the intended occasion to demonstrate the resulting system to the world. [D15](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Sources-and-documents#supplied-background-documents)

This opens large questions about representation, participation, identity, public authority, deliberation and accountability as AI becomes more capable. They deserve an open argument and a lawful public process. A local workbench supplies a place to learn how people use evidence and participation tools in ordinary decisions before drawing national conclusions.
