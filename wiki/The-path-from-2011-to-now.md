## 2011 and the systems question

After the south-east Queensland floods, I was investigating disaster resilience and the relationships between people, infrastructure and institutions. The Bruce Mau visit was part of that period. My recollection of meeting you belongs here, in November 2011.

The State Library itself had reopened in February 2011 after the floods. The setting was part of the regional experience of disruption and recovery. [S06](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Sources-and-documents#s06)

## The 2012-13 legal reading

The intensive Australian-law reading came later, in late 2012 and the summer of 2012-13. I began with the fine print around my own life: documents, licences, accounts, forms and the legal structures behind them. I gathered and read legislation to understand the rules and institutions I was participating in.

The [public archive](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/australian-law-2012-lukes-relevance/index.html) includes 61 documents, including multiple volumes of major Acts. The significance is the change in how I approached systems: reading their actual terms, tracing their relationships and asking how people might understand and improve them.

The current legal-engine prototype draws on that historical collection. Its development into a maintained contemporary legal service is a separate piece of work. [The P4A chapter](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/P4A-law-and-democratic-participation) explains the distinction and the longer constitutional aim.

## Learning, participation and early interfaces

[IChooseInfinity](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/open-record-luke-nathan-hayes), the Next Great Conversation and Gamify Democracy explored learning, public questions and participation. The Constitutional Matrix of Participation followed in July 2014.

A 2014 account records a visit to The Edge at the State Library to discuss the [Verse Box](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/aura-verse-box) idea. The proposal brought together a public computer interface, conversation, media and shared questions. Today's Verse Box revisits that public introduction through the current Aura architecture. The hardware and software have changed; the interest in making participation understandable has continued.

## Actual local contributions

In 2014 I transcribed oral histories for the North Stradbroke Island / Minjerribah Historical Museum. My [work record](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/open-record-luke-nathan-hayes) includes that volunteering. My grandmother, Constance Millar, was alive and involved with the museum during that period. Her contribution to community life is part of my personal connection to the place.

I also worked on the Quandamooka Festival website and events and volunteered at Island Vibe in 2015-16. The Island Vibe work included assisting the site manager and the mentor programme. The festival website work extended across three years; I remember receiving about $300 for that contribution.

## Aura and the wider architecture

The July 2023 Aura presentation already connected a personal intelligence interface with shared cultural participation and values inquiry. Its gathering proposal used three continuous days organised around past, now and next. That edition targeted an earlier Live Aid anniversary. The current anchor is the 50th anniversary in July 2035. [D01](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Sources-and-documents#supplied-background-documents)

Travel and research in India formed another part of this period. My 2024 CV was prepared after returning to Australia. The public [Open Record](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/open-record-luke-nathan-hayes) and [Man and Mind](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/luke-nathan-hayes-man-and-mind/index.html) pages provide more of the professional, creative and research history.

## The recent body of work

By October 2026 the public collection included personal-intelligence interfaces, civic workbenches, place studies, project and grant tools, media plans, material research, resilience proposals and creative worlds. The [Project Atlas](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/project-atlas/projects.html) helps navigate that collection. A repository creation date identifies a particular software build; it does not establish when the originating idea began.

Most of my current effort has gone into research, software, writing, system design and creative work, often in solitude. Strange But True is the sole-trader identity around that activity. I have not operated the market stalls imagined in some earlier planning material.
