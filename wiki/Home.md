# A Massive Change and a Fair Go

## A whole system overview and an invitation to Paul Bishop

Paul,

I remember meeting you at Bruce Mau's State Library of Queensland visit in November 2011. I was exploring disaster resilience after the south-east Queensland floods. You interviewed me with your video recorder. That conversation sits near the beginning of a much longer period of investigating how our systems work and designing alternatives.

This wiki brings that work together and opens a practical conversation about its local leadership. My main invitation is for you to help carry the Redlands expression of the programme, convene the people who want to develop it, and explore a working relationship that gives the work continuity while I develop the wider architecture and network.

I have thought seriously about the possible short mayoral term if a vacancy leads to a by-election. I believe there is substance here for a winning public case. Earning that mandate would involve real organising and public scrutiny. My preference is to preserve the freedom to research, create, travel, earn a living and build the wider relationships. A good local handover would put that preference into practice.

The proposed partnership also includes a personal invitation to explore Aura Genesis, the intensive programme combining hyperbaric oxygen therapy, reflection and the proposed Truth Engine, and to engage with P4A's civic work. Those invitations are explained in their own chapters. They are significant parts of the conversation, with their own practical arrangements and choices.

## The purpose

Joyful Responsible Abundance is the guiding idea. It asks whether growing capability makes life more worthwhile, whether we take responsibility for its consequences, and whether people share in its benefits.

**Mutual Futures is central.** It connects productive ownership, business succession, learning, income, care and time for life during the AI and automation transition. Around it sit personal intelligence, cooperative work, community computing, cultural production, democratic participation and physical infrastructure.

The long horizon includes underground settlements with the productive systems to support successive generations, shared Earth and space observations, preparation for severe disruptions, and Australia helping countries cooperate across their differences. The proposed GAJRA Earth network connects locally shaped associations through practical work and a continuing inquiry into futures worth inhabiting.

For me, July 2035 is a key point in that long game: Live Aid's 50th anniversary, connected with three days of art, music and public reflection on past, present and future. Brisbane 2032 and Australia's coming sequence of total solar eclipses offer other shared occasions along the way.

## Reading the status of the work

There is a substantial body of published writing, websites, software workbenches, creative work and research. There is also earlier hands-on community work and a number of direct conversations with public representatives. The cooperative, physical installations and wider service network still need their participating people, organisations, resources and agreements. The [current position](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Current-position-and-open-decisions) chapter makes those distinctions explicit.

My account of our first meeting and the other private meetings is personal recollection. Dated public facts have a separate [source register](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Sources-and-documents). A proposal is written as a proposal; an established activity has its actual record. Keeping those records clear makes the ambition easier to examine and carry forward.

This is an invitation to discuss responsibility, participation and a workable relationship. It is written so you, other councillors, community members and prospective collaborators have enough context to assess the whole picture.
