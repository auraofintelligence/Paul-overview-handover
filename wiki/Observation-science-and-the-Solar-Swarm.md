The research layer asks how people could observe, compare and debate major Earth and space processes more openly. It connects public scientific records, proposed sensor systems, simulations and a culture of correction.

## The Virtual Solar Swarm

The [Virtual Solar Swarm](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/virtual-solar-swarm/) is a public mission-design and learning studio. Its present catalogue contains 384 bodies, with models for instruments, orbits, launch windows, fleet allocation, communications and replacement cycles. It lets a reader change assumptions and see how a proposed observing network changes.

The originating question was put to students at the SDNx Innovation Lab in Kolhapur, Maharashtra, in June 2019: if transport and cost were solved elsewhere, what sensor packages would you send to the solar system's most interesting objects? The present studio is a later independent design exercise. The people who attended that event are not thereby endorsers of the current proposal.

The current site distinguishes measured source data from mission design assumptions. It describes its physics as suitable for teaching and comparison, rather than spacecraft operations. No fleet described there has been funded, built or launched through this project.

The earlier [Solar Swarm](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/virtual-solar-swarm/) report explored roughly 1,500-2,000 satellites around about 200 objects and a network of solar observation nodes. Those figures describe one study configuration. The current interactive work gives readers choices rather than treating that configuration as a decided procurement. [D08](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Sources-and-documents#supplied-background-documents)

Communication obeys light travel time. A solar-system network has delays, bandwidth limits, conjunctions and outages. Local autonomy and a clear control hierarchy become part of the design.

## Space weather and the micronova inquiry

[Micronova and Excursions](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/micronova-and-excursions/sitemap.html) is a public inquiry into claims about solar activity, geomagnetic change, excursions and severe events. Its current audit takes objections seriously. It identifies weak recurrence and timing claims, unresolved mechanisms, failed predictions and erroneous magnetic-decline figures in some older material.

That is the position to carry into the handover. Documented solar flares, coronal mass ejections and geomagnetic disturbances have observable consequences. A claim of a periodic catastrophic solar event requires its own mechanism, evidence and predictions. Similar names used for different stellar phenomena do not settle the proposed solar mechanism.

## The Sensorium and public scientific debate

The Web3 Sensorium proposal explores shared records for observation, scientific discussion and review. A sensor record needs its instrument, location or orbit, time, calibration, uncertainty and processing history. A conclusion needs a traceable path back to those records. [D09](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Sources-and-documents#supplied-background-documents)

Related space-weather and earthquake material explores automated alerts and a goal of highly specific forecasting. Those are research aims. The material does not demonstrate a validated ability to forecast an earthquake within an hour and 100 kilometres. [D18](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Sources-and-documents#supplied-background-documents)
