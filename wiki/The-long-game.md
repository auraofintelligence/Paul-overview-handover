The programme has several horizons. There are useful things to do in the coming months, a council term to understand, the international attention around Brisbane 2032, a major cultural date in July 2035 and a much longer resilience and human development inquiry.

These dates help organise attention and preparation. The work at each stage still needs its own purpose, participating people and resources.

## July 2035

The 50th anniversary of Live Aid is a key date in my long game. The proposal brings together art, music and three days of reflection on past, present and future. The public process would invite people to explore what Joyful Responsible Abundance means, what they want to carry forward and what they want to make possible.

The three-part gathering appeared in earlier Aura material with a July 2025 target. That was an earlier proposal, rather than an event shown to have taken place. The current horizon in this handover is July 2035. [D01](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Sources-and-documents#supplied-background-documents) [D04](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/Paul-overview-handover/wiki/Sources-and-documents#supplied-background-documents)

The anniversary provides a cultural reference and a date. It does not establish an affiliation with the Live Aid organisations or permission to use their identity. The proposed gathering would have its own organisers, programme and agreements.

## The eclipse sequence

I describe the eclipses as etheric signs because they carry personal symbolic meaning for me. A rare shared event can bring people's attention to a longer span of time and prompt reflection on their place in it. That is a statement of meaning, distinct from an astronomical explanation or evidence of a predicted catastrophe.

The 2037 Brisbane eclipse falling on 13 July has a particular resonance with the 2035 gathering's anniversary date. It is a calendar connection. People would be free to take meaning from it in their own way.

Practical participation could include science, film, music, intergenerational learning and ways for people in different places to share the occasion. Each local programme would need accurate information and appropriate arrangements for the actual event.

## Brisbane 2032 and the years leading to it

The Games offer a visible horizon for work already useful to the people here. Accessible participation, community media, practical learning, better public evidence and a credible shared-ownership example would each have value before visitors arrive and after they leave.

The proposed Olympic and P4A demonstrations are my programme ideas. Their development would involve actual hosts and organisers. A strong local record would make a later invitation easier to assess.

## Beyond a single term or event

[Future of Life 2045](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/Future-of-Life-2045/) preserves an earlier authored future, developed around a 2022 contest entry and a longer inquiry going back further. It is useful as a record of direction, rather than a forecast.

The handover seeks continuity across these horizons. A council relationship can help the local work grow roots. An operating group, useful enterprises and a public record give it a life beyond any one officeholder, event or founder's availability.
